## 2. Strategic Focus & Selected Candidate Line

Following the comparative evaluation of two distinct public health challenges, our team established a **justified provisional preference for Candidate Line 2**:

* **Selected Candidate Line**: **Mental Health Emergencies, Suicidal Ideation & Psychiatric Bed Bottlenecks**
* **Core Research Question**:
  > *"To what extent does the territorial disparity between acute mental health emergency demand (SADU: ICD-10 R45.8, X60-X84, F30-F48) and specialized short-stay psychiatric bed capacity (REM20: Functional Area Psiquiatría) create systemic bottlenecks across Chile's 29 Health Services?"*
* **Policy Alignment & Legal Mandate**:
  - *Law 21.331 (2021) on Mental Health Protection*: Mandating parity integration of psychiatric beds into general public hospitals and prohibiting segregated asylum care.
  - *National Health Strategy (ENS) 2030*: Target of a 15% reduction in youth and adolescent suicide rates.
  - *MINSAL National Mental Health Plan*: Chronic budgetary gap (~2.4% allocated vs. WHO recommended >5.0%).
* **Primary Key Performance Indicator (KPI)**:
  $$\text{Mental Health Stress Ratio} = \frac{\text{Acute Mental Health Emergency Visits (SADU: Codes 35, 37, 39, 40)}}{\text{Available Psychiatric Bed-Days (REM20: Functional Areas Psiquiatría)}}$$
* **Key Empirical Discovery**: 
  Extreme territorial inequality across Chile's 29 Health Services, with regions like *Del Maule* (Stress Ratio: 1.83) and *Talcahuano* (1.62) facing severe holding bottlenecks, contrasted with an absolute absence of child/adolescent psychiatric beds across 18 of the 29 Health Services.

All steps are deterministic, project-relative, and fully reproducible:

The repository already contains the verified reduced files in `data/`. To re-extract them from raw files:

Open and run the notebook interactively in Jupyter:

```bash
python -m nbclient analysis/workshop1.ipynb
```

### Step 3: Re-Compile the Standalone PDF Report
To compile the 7-page self-contained PDF report with embedded figures and tables:
```bash
python report/build_pdf_report.py
```
